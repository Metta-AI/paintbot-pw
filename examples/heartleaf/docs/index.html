A POLYWORLD EXAMPLE GAME

Nine villagers, one week, one bell. Gather the morning vegetables, talk your neighbours into coming over, and be inside the right house when the clock strikes six — because dinner is the only thing that scores.

The week

Days run 9:00 to 21:00 on an accelerated clock. Every morning each garden grows one random vegetable; a gatherer takes everything the plot holds and it stays bare until tomorrow. At exactly 18:00 every house holds its dinner tally at once: a party is valid when the owner is home with at least one visitor.

  • Hosting: the host banks items carried × visitor count, then the pantry feeds everyone for three bite rounds — and empties.
  • Eating: a vegetable you have never tasted this week is worth 3, a repeat is worth 1. Guests eat for free.
  • Loneliness: anyone alone, or outside, scores nothing that night.

Invitations are real commands — a wave within three tiles declares you a host — but nothing binds anyone. Attendance is purely who is physically inside which house when the bell rings.

The village

The map is generated from a seed: a ring of nine houses around a stone plaza, dirt roads from every door, three garden plots beside each house, and a forest closing in from the map edge. Houses have no interiors — a villager who steps inside becomes a little portrait floating over the roof.

The bots

Each villager is one BASIC program deciding once per game minute: gather, walk, invite, accept, enter, exit. The reference villager plays the strong plan from the game this borrows its ideas from — gather all day, host by rotation, guest everywhere else, and never be alone at six. 

nim r examples/heartleaf/heartleaf.nim --bot examples/heartleaf/players/base.bas:9

nim r -d:headless examples/heartleaf/heartleaf.nim --seed 1988 \
  --bot examples/heartleaf/players/base.bas:9 \
  --record examples/heartleaf/replays/demo.replay

nim r examples/heartleaf/heartleaf.nim --replay examples/heartleaf/replays/demo.replay
Every game is deterministic: same seed, same bots, same week — recorded to a replay that verifies bit-exactly on playback.
